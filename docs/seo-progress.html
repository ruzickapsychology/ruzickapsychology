From overview to next action

Choose a workflow, then a category. Ready items have a specific bounded action; In progress is reserved for work actually started. No implementation has been started by this triage.

  • Ready: selected, scoped and independently executable.
  • Needs scoping: investigate the value or define the deliverable before committing.
  • Blocked: requires a fact, approval, account access or another external dependency.
  • Backlog: plausible future work, not a current commitment.
  • Archived: declined, redundant or consolidated; reason and source retained. Not counted as completed.
  • Done: previously verified work, kept in its own view.
  • Reference: facts to resolve, process notes, monitoring prompts, metadata examples and expansion ideas; not tickets.

Partial, Remaining and Unverified describe the original audit evidence, independently of workflow. Importance is the PDF’s rating, not our execution order. Ready items show the scoped action first; expand to see the original recommendation and acceptance criteria.

Promote work into Ready only after its value, scope, dependencies and completion check are clear. Move it to In progress when implementation starts. Record verification before Done. The progress bar includes historical Done work and the accepted queue, not deferred work or resources.
