# Before we meet — exploration 04

A folded cream note on the About page opens into the first two paragraphs of Christina's existing CMS biography. The paper has a fine grain, a slanted crease, layered edges, and an embossed flower seal. Opening reveals the note with a short, gentle fold movement.

The content comes from `about.intro` and the author from `about.heading`. The complete biography and the original philosophy band remain available, as do credentials, office information, and the consultation action. The note deliberately offers a short excerpt of the introduction; it does not invent a separate personal letter. No new personal statements or clinical claims were authored for this exploration.

## Review

Open `/about#before-we-meet` and activate **Unfold the note**. The whole cover is an accessible native disclosure target. Press Enter or Space to open/close it; it also works without JavaScript. Reduced motion removes the unfolding movement. There is no autoplay, hover-only content, tracking, persistence, or new dependency.

The committed images show the folded and open note at 375, 768, and 1440 pixels. `tests/e2e/before-we-meet.spec.ts` exercises Enter/Space, focus retention, reduced motion, overflow, and JavaScript-disabled operation.

## Engineering

- Server-rendered `FoldedNote` composes shared typography and the existing brand flower; its children use PortableContent for the CMS excerpt.
- Native `<details>` supplies keyboard operation and expanded/collapsed semantics.
- Only local CSS handles the paper texture, seal, fold, and short animation.
- About retains runtime CMS content as its only content source.
- CSS Module declarations are generated by the repository toolchain.

This PR is an independent visual exploration and is not merged or deployed.
