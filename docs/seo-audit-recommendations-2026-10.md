## Tracking conventions

- Stable IDs identify individual actions. **Done** = verified completed implementation (or a documented guardrail/decision where that is the item). **Partial** = some requirements met, with the gap identified. **Remaining** = requested website work absent or explicitly still pending. **Unverified** = completion cannot be established from this comparison, often because it requires external accounts, private approvals or recurring logs. Checkboxes follow workflow: Ready/In progress are unchecked, Done is checked, and other queues have no checkbox. Historical source assessments remain independent; completing a narrower action does not rewrite the original audit evidence. Each item has a dated evidence note.
- Sections 01–30 map to the PDF’s numbered findings; sections 31–40 capture extra details elsewhere. Repeated recommendations are consolidated, with source pages retained.
- `PAGE-01`–`PAGE-30` and `BLOG-01`–`BLOG-28` are future page and article resources, not tasks; `AI-01`–`AI-12` are recurring prompt checks, not one-time completion claims. `META-01`–`META-07` preserve proposed metadata pairs, and `KW-01`–`KW-41` are keyword reference resources. All Resource items have no task checkboxes and are excluded from progress; their earlier coverage assessments remain for context. Additional process, decision, monitoring and metadata references are explicitly grouped.
- The complete page-numbered text extraction at the end preserves exact proposed metadata, all 41 keyword targets and estimates, schema templates, llms.txt draft, compliance questions, baseline evidence, limitations and wording variants. It supplements the checklist rather than adding new actions.
- Source inconsistencies are preserved: proposed title lengths do not always match sample titles, and proposed PMH-C wording conflicts with the source’s caution against using that abbreviation before certification. Resolve these when implementing.
